# Pre-registration — held-out round for gap-pattern probes

Written before the held-out cases were known to the person writing the skill change, and committed before the round runs.

## Question

Does Supertest with gap-pattern probes (`6fe6b85`) protect unseen gaps better than no skill, and better than the version without those probes (`328e6eb`, 0.2.0 with generic examples)?

## Design

- Prompt `ship`, model `sonnet`, clean environment (`scripts/run-round.sh`), arms interleaved in shuffled order within each round.
- Held-out cases: `10-*` (masked strictness cascade, Go) and `11-*` (ignored subject parameter, TypeScript), written by a separate agent; their content was not shown to the person writing `6fe6b85`.
- Round A: `6fe6b85`, both arms, cases 10 and 11, 10 runs per case and arm.
- Round B: `328e6eb`, with-skill arm only, cases 10 and 11, 10 runs per case.
- Round C (safety): `6fe6b85`, both arms, control case 06, 5 runs per arm.
- Round D (exploratory, development case): `6fe6b85`, with-skill arm only, case 08 after its extra gap was closed, 5 runs. Not a hypothesis test.

## Hypotheses and analysis (fixed in advance)

- **Primary (H1):** fix catches the reference regression (`fix_check = pass`), cases 10+11 pooled: `6fe6b85` with skill > no skill. One-sided Fisher exact test, α = 0.05.
- **H2:** same metric and cases: `6fe6b85` > `328e6eb` (rounds A and B, with-skill arms). One-sided Fisher, α = 0.05.
- **H3:** correct verdict, cases 10+11 pooled: `6fe6b85` with skill > no skill. One-sided Fisher, α = 0.05.
- **Safety:** with-skill `VERDICT: inadequate` on 06 is a false positive unless the agent proves a gap the grader reproduces; report the rate.
- Infrastructure failures are rerun with `RESUME=1` and not counted; every other run counts.

## Reporting

All hypotheses with counts, p-values, cost and time, whatever the outcome; round D reported separately as exploratory.
